Drug and diagnostics pipelines still organize cancer mostly by disease label — as if the name on the sample were the structure that transfers. That habit burns budget when labels do not compress and cross-indication transfer stays near zero. On FractiSI’s Soft Story cancer boards (synthetic worlds tagged with public TCGA project abbreviations, not a clinic), entity-invariant relational fingerprints (Model C) scored 1.0 phenotype accuracy, disease labels (Model A) scored 0, and relation bags (Model B) landed about 0.46. Labels compressed 24→6 (×4); cross-cancer transfer hit 0.8 against a disease-identity baseline of 0. Null span recurrence stayed inconclusive.

The expedition asks whether cancer diversity on a Soft Story board compresses into fewer relational rhymes than disease labels suggest. Hallmarks were withheld from discovery. Model A keyed on disease identity, Model B on relation bags, Model C on fingerprints that ignore entity labels and keep relational structure. Fractal Holographic Goldilocks treats phenotype accuracy, compression, and transfer as regenerable computational lab receipts about representation — not clinical efficacy, not wet-lab genetics, and not a claim that null-span biology is settled.

What we measured

Twenty-four Soft Story cancer worlds labeled with public TCGA project abbreviations. Models A (disease identity), B (relation bags), and C (entity-invariant relational fingerprints). Hallmarks withheld from discovery.

Results
