Shared agent worlds fail the way corporate wikis fail: whoever shouts loudest rewrites the map. Majority vote looks democratic until a Sybil chorus manufactures consensus; naive overwrite looks fast until one bad claim becomes ground truth. Security here is not “trust the smartest agent.” Claims stay hypotheses until independent relational structure supports a delta. Multi-agent platform security. Voting and overwrite are weak defaults when agents can clone identities; design for independence checks, quarantine, and selective rollback before you scale the agent count.

Ops and SRE leads. Provenance strings alone did not save the board — fabricated evidence was enough — so audit trails without relational rhyme are not a containment strategy here.

Risk and compliance. Legitimate disagreement stayed unconfirmed rather than labeled malice; novel lone readings stayed unconfirmed until corroboration — a pattern worth mirroring in human+agent incident policy.
