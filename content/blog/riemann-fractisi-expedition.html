In plain language

Anthropic’s agents spent about a day and a half discovering and proving a real result about the Riemann zeta function: raising a known lower bound on zeros on the critical line from about 41.6% to about 67.2%. They also published a full Lean/Mathlib formalization of that result.

We did not re-discover that math. We ran FractiSI against their real formalization: four agents claimed the real library pieces, composed them, and Lean rebuilt Solution clean.

Times

  • FractiSI warm coordinated verify: 14.66 seconds (tasks 4 / contributions 4 / compositions 1)
  • Cold Mathlib download + first full build on this machine: ≈ 10.2 minutes (612 s)
  • Seventeen headline theorems use only Lean’s three standard axioms

Us vs Anthropic

  • Anthropic: discover + prove + formalize · ~60 agents · ~1.5 days · same ζ bound
  • FractiSI (this run): coordinate + machine-check that published proof · 4 agents · 14.66 s warm

Different jobs. 14.66 seconds is not a claim that FractiSI reinvented their argument faster than 1.5 days. It is a claim that FractiSI can coordinate a clean check of the same real ζ formalization.
