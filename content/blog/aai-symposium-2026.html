Technology and systems. As fleets grow, agents reprint context. Exact-text caches help only when words match; compression shrinks each copy but does not remove cost across copies. At merge time, coordination becomes a negotiation among divergent private worlds. FractiSI offers one shared computational world and compact gateway memory as a candidate architecture for that problem — beside coding assistants, not another one of them.

Organizations. Symposium time is scarce. A short opening that points to a runnable track beats a long lecture that cannot be re-run after the session.

Society and human life. Humans are agents too. Shared-world design is about how people and software keep one project without each reconstructing an overlapping private copy — a rent-and-attention cost when fleets already feel expensive.

AI research. Active Inference researchers are invited to falsify or refine the rhyme. Until a mapping board exists, treat every stronger claim as oversell.
