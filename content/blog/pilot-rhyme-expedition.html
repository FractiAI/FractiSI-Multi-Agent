What we tested

One hundred six-step missions. Same role structure every time. Different wording every time. Five arms: flat peers, shared text without roles, Director/Pilot with private copies, Director/Pilot with an exact-text cache, and Pilot Rhyme.

Results

  • Flat peers and shared text: 0 of 100
  • All Director/Pilot arms: 100 of 100
  • Exact-text role cache: 0 hits when wording differed
  • Pilot Rhyme store: 22% smaller than exact cache, 80% smaller than private copies

Candidates on sky and genome panels

On the same public theaters as the other rhymes, Pilot Rhyme listed 692 named long-range partners that flat peers missed — exoplanets, NEOs, deep-sky objects, microwave multipoles, and demo exon/chrM pairs. Those are candidates for follow-up, not finished discoveries.

Plain reading

Roles matter for finding the answer. Rhyme matters for keeping the work small when the words change. That is the same pattern we saw against exact caches on protein and GPS traffic.

Lab result. Private Director/Pilot copies also scored 100 of 100, so accuracy versus flat is the role structure. Rhyme’s edge is size. No LingBot weights. Not CapEx.
