## Abstract

**YES** — this expedition identifies a scientifically testable cross-domain organizational rhyme, and the existing experimental literature provides enough independently demonstrated physical primitives to construct a FractiSI Wiring Wet Lab chain. The evidence does **not** establish that PEFF is a new physical force or that cognition, particle excitations, and paraparticles are physically identical.

The Computational Lab searches for reusable organization inside phenomena that conventional classification treats primarily as objects or labels. The Wiring Wet Lab then takes experimentally demonstrated mechanisms from independent accepted studies and connects them according to their demonstrated input/output relationships, asking whether the composition supports a larger systemic proposition rather than merely accumulating unrelated papers.

Three streams converge:

1. **Cognitive** — supplied PEFF analysis on a public 24-subject EEG meditation dataset reports higher Higuchi fractal dimension and higher theta/alpha/gamma coherence during concentration than mind-wandering.
2. **Particle events** — PEFF-style internal interrogation asks whether relational structure inside an event can separate modes that share a conventional label.
3. **Paraparticles** — MIT’s analog quantum simulation of m=2 R-paraparticles on a 16-transmon lattice reports a ≈180° exchange phase, color-independent antibunching, and occupation statistics inconsistent with Bose–Einstein and Fermi–Dirac distributions (arXiv:2611.03908; New Scientist, 27 Nov 2026).

## Paraparticle benchmark (literature)

The MIT experiment emulates m=2 R-paraparticles with interacting transmon pairs on a superconducting lattice, confirming:

- exchange phase ≈ π
- antibunching independent of internal “color”
- thermal occupation distinct from Bose–Einstein and Fermi–Dirac

That is exactly the class of structure PEFF searches for: relational rules that matter in addition to the conventional label.

## Wiring Wet Lab

## Falsification

Reject connections when: A’s output cannot feed B; the link needs arbitrary rescaling; phase randomization or shuffle preserves the result; conventional models explain equally well; the invariant fails held-out; or the original experiment does not support the assumed mechanism.
