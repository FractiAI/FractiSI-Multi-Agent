## Abstract

**There should be no reason to spend the time and money when it has already been done.**

Classical wet labs invent new measurements. Catalogs score known edges. The **wiring wet lab** is a new form: it treats published assays, clinical series, and public spectra as reusable reagents, then assembles them into a relationship edge the catalog does not yet list — with kill switches. The product is not a repeated assay. The product is the **wire**.

This paper titles that form, argues AI and FractiSI multiagent Rhyming World Models make it routine, defines definitional gates, and points to regenerable peers: NPY↔SOD1, APP↔DNMT3A, WMAP residual lag wiring, the combo protein-folding+LLM+Pilot expedition, and bridge thickening. The framework is Soft Story / application companion — not Infinite Octaves ENGINE_SHELF — until a separate pin review says otherwise.

## Primary research question

Can a **wiring wet lab** be defined as a first-class wet-lab form — immediate, no new assay spend when the pieces already exist — that is falsifiable and routinely executable with AI + FractiSI Rhyming World Models?

## Background — the missing middle

Assay discovery is familiar. Catalog discovery is familiar. Between them sits a quiet failure mode that burns assay time and grant money. Pieces already exist in the literature; a rhyme board already recovers a pair; the catalog still shows empty; nobody files the relationship as a first-class object with falsifiers. The cost is duplicated assays and delayed follow-up.

Systematic reviews help humans read. They rarely emit regenerable JSON gates. Knowledge-graph completion maximizes edges. It rarely celebrates catalog silence as a novelty condition. The wiring wet lab sits in the middle with a different virtue: **reuse**.

## Thesis: the best wet lab for the already-done case

If three labs already published the measurements, and the association database is still empty for the pair you care about, **re-running the same spend is the wrong default**. The right default is a wiring wet lab:

1. Recover the relationship on a rhyme board (coupling).
2. Confirm the catalog edge is empty (or provisional with named kills).
3. Prove the catalog lane works with a positive control.
4. Assemble existing empirical pieces into pre-registered roles.
5. Publish kill conditions before celebration.
6. Cite the assays — do not pretend the assembling team ran them.

That is still experimental method. The bench is the literature + catalogs + rhyme world. The reagent cost is citation and compute, not a second grant to re-measure what is already on the shelf.

## Why this is a new form (not a softer old form)

| Form | What you buy | When it is wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Physical wet lab | New measurement | When the measurement already exists and you only need the *relationship* |
| Catalog curation | Scored edge in STRING / OpenTargets / etc. | When the edge is silent but the pieces exist |
| Narrative review | Human synthesis | When you need regenerable gates and kill switches |
| **Wiring wet lab** | Falsifiable relationship edge assembled from existing spend | When no pieces exist — then you still need a physical lab |

The wiring wet lab does not replace physical labs. It **stops forcing physical labs to redo completed spend** before a relationship can be filed.

## Method (definitional gates)

A claim may be labeled a **wiring wet lab discovery** only if:

1. A rhyme board recovers the relationship with measurable coupling.
2. The relevant catalog edge is empty (or explicitly provisional with named kills).
3. A positive control proves the catalog lane works.
4. ≥N existing empirical pieces assemble into a path with pre-registered roles.
5. Kill conditions are written before celebration.
6. The claim does **not** pretend the assembling team ran the underlying assays — it cites them.

## Discussion

### Immediate reuse as the virtue

The wiring wet lab’s virtue is **immediate reuse**. Follow-up work should not wait on five more years of duplicate assays when the papers already exist and the catalog is silent. AI does not invent the original assay. FractiSI does not replace STRING. Together they make the assembly and falsification loop cheap enough that “already done” becomes the first experiment, not the last excuse.

### Relation to FractiSI Multi-Agent

Pilot Rhyme and shared-world features make long-range partners recoverable. The wiring wet lab is how product-facing FractiSI research turns those recoveries into **filed, killable relationship objects** instead of endless discovery catalogs.

### Failure modes

- Celebrating a single rhyme hit without emptiness + assembly.
- Treating same-chromosome recovery as disease wiring (bridge thickening’s chr21 lesson).
- Upgrading Soft Story into unfinished clinical or CMB laws.
- Skipping positive controls so emptiness becomes an API blackout story.

### Limitations

Framework Soft Story · not ENGINE_SHELF · not a claim every wire is true biology/physics · depends on peer suite honesty · Vercel deploy state is operational, not epistemic.

## Worked definition (one paragraph)

A **wiring wet lab** is a falsifiable experimental form whose reagents are existing public empirical pieces: it recovers a catalog-empty (or provisionally novel) relationship edge via rhyme geometry, assembles cited wet-lab / clinical / spectrum roles under pre-registered gates, proves the catalog lane with a positive control, and refuses to demand re-spend of time and money on assays already completed.
