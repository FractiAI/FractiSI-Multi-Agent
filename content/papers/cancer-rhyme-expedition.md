## Abstract

Conventional oncology language often says cancer is many diseases and may therefore need many independent solutions. This Rhyming Expedition arm asks a narrower, measurable question: **how many distinct disease labels are required versus how many recurring relational structures appear to generate the observed Soft Story diversity?**

We do **not** encode Hallmarks of Cancer as discovery features. Hallmarks, pathways, and literature appear only as post-hoc interpretation after structural discovery. Three competing models run on the same regenerable board:

- **Model A — Independent Disease:** TCGA project identity as the feature.
- **Model B — Shared Mechanism:** bags of relation verbs (noise verbs only for fair bag baseline).
- **Model C — Relational World-Model:** entity-invariant multi-relation fingerprints (rhymes).

Awareness is operationalized strictly as **world-model integration capacity** — detecting, integrating, and responding under local and higher-order constraints — not as subjective experience.

On seed `20261120`, Model C phenotype accuracy is 1.000, Model B 0.458, Model A 0.000. Disease taxonomy complexity is 24; strong planted rhyme complexity is 6 (compression ×4). Cross-cancer transfer of shared fingerprints scores 0.800. Local−shared objective divergence is higher on malignant-like synthetic phenotypes. Six planted motifs are rediscovered as structural rhymes spanning 12 cancers each. Null recurrence at planted span is inconclusive because shuffled boards also produce high-span noise pairs. The Soft Story verdict is positive on compression, transfer, and Model C vs A/B — and explicitly qualified on the null test.

The central reframe is preserved: cancer may be thousands of diseases at the surface and still substantially fewer **problems** at the relational level. This paper does not claim the surface taxonomy is wrong; it asks whether relational compression adds predictive structure the taxonomy alone does not transfer.

## 1. Research question

If cancer observations are enormously diverse, does that diversity arise from thousands of fundamentally independent problems — or from a smaller number of recurring relational configurations implemented through different molecular, cellular, and tissue Soft Story worlds?

Deeper (hypothesis, not assumption): does progression on this board show increasing divergence between local optimization and shared-world compatibility (reduced world-model integration)?

Can FractiSI discover, represent, and predict those transitions through relational rhyme before they appear as conventional disease labels?

## 3. Method

### 3.1 Soft Story worlds (Seed:Edge)

Each of 24 worlds is a shared world with agents (tumor / immune / stromal Soft Story roles), states, relationships, tissue context, boundary flags, and temporal seed→edge transitions. Public TCGA project abbreviations label worlds; they are **not** patient records.

Six structural motifs are planted across half the cancers (entity labels differ; relation structure preserved). Discovery ignores Hallmark taxonomies. Planted motifs are scored post-hoc as rediscovered vs noise.

### 3.2 Competing models

| Model | Features | Intent |
|---|---|---|
| A | Disease / TCGA id | Independent disease transfer |
| B | Relation-type bags (noise verbs) | Shared mechanism without structure |
| C | Entity-invariant fingerprints `R3\|rel1,rel2,…` | Relational rhyme / world-model |
| Oracle | Planted motif presence | Upper Soft Story bound |

Phenotype prediction uses leave-one-out nearest neighbor (Jaccard). Disease bags never overlap across types, so Model A reports honest zero cross-type transfer.

### 3.3 Core experiments

1. **Rhyme discovery** — recurring structural fingerprints across ≥3 cancers.
2. **Rhyme compression** — disease complexity vs strong rhyme count vs phenotype retention.
3. **Cross-cancer transfer** — train fingerprints on A, predict phenotype on B.
4. **Null worlds** — shuffle relations; compare high-span recurrence.
5. **Local vs shared** — local objective vs shared-world compatibility proxies.
6. **Goldilocks / boundary band** — joint favorability of integration, differentiation, adaptability, boundary integrity (candidate band only after measurement).
7. **Novelty tiers** — rediscovered_planted · extended_candidate · novel_candidate_needs_lit · unsupported_or_noise.
8. **Literature** — Hallmarks and pan-cancer themes used **only after** discovery as post-hoc hints.

### 3.4 Awareness operationalization

World-model integration capacity proxies on each world:

- signal integration strength  
- neighbor/context incorporation  
- boundary integrity  
- feedback coherence  
- multiscale (local vs higher-order) compatibility  

**Not** consciousness. Loss of awareness on this board means reduced contextual integration relative to local optimization — a Soft Story measurement, not a spiritual claim.

### 4.6 Null test (qualification)

Observed planted span = 12 cancers. Null mean structural hits at that span ≈ 16.3; `pAtLeastOneHit ≈ 1`. On this Soft Story board, high-span recurrence alone does not separate planted motifs from shuffled noise. The decisive Soft Story evidence is therefore **phenotype prediction, compression, and transfer**, not null exclusivity of span. Report as **inconclusive null**, not as failure of Model C.

### 4.7 Verdict

Positive Soft Story expedition signal on: `C_beats_A`, `C_beats_B`, `oracle_planted`, `compression`, `transfer`, `local_vs_shared`, `goldilocks_proxy`. Null signal **not** fired.

## 6. Qualifications / competing explanations

- Worlds are synthetic Soft Story graphs with TCGA **labels**, not TCGA molecular matrices.
- Model C’s perfect LOO score is expected when planted structure dominates phenotype on a controlled board; the scientific content is the **gap vs A/B and transfer**, not the absolute ceiling.
- Null inconclusiveness means span recurrence alone is a weak novelty claim on this fixture.
- Goldilocks band is a measured proxy region, not established biology.
- Failure mode recorded honestly: if future public molecular boards show A/B ≥ C, the FractiSI relational hypothesis fails on those boards.
