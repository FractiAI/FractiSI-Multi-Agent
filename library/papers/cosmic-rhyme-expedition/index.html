Abstract

E9–E12 showed that a relational awareness board can recover structure a linear pass misses across proteins, knowledge, macro series, and complementary collaboration. This expedition asks whether the same grammar opens a protein-folding telescope on the cosmos: real public catalogs and spectra, open-ended motif search, residual physics search after selection removal, and the same packing grammar used for LLMs. The question is not what the Universe is doing. It is what becomes visible when cosmic observations are represented as relationships — including as language packs.

Primary research question

Can the same relational-awareness method that revealed hidden structure in proteins, knowledge, and macroeconomic data reveal previously unrecognized super-patterns in the cosmos — and does that rhyme survive residual physics search and language packing?

Experimental rhyme

Protein sequence → couplings → fold. Knowledge documents → source relationships → answer. Macroeconomy series → teleconnections → structure. Cosmos observations → cosmic relationships → ?. The question mark is the discovery target. We do not tell the system to find filaments, voids, or dark-matter maps.

What we found, and how novel it is

  1. Method travels (most novel for this program). Feature boards and language packs both recover long-range partners on public sky files.
  2. Catalog fingerprints (moderate observational novelty). Open motif search kept survey-history and catalog-geometry couplings without being told to hunt selection effects.
  3. Candidate physics (qualified). C4 residual lags 11/14 — candidates under protocol, not confirmed laws.
  4. Not confirmed new physics. No new particle, force, filament map, or Universe ontology. AWARE wins because it uses a ranked board LINEAR never sees.

What this does claim

If relational awareness is a general property of intelligence, increasing the relational representation of cosmic data — including as language packs — should expose structure that is difficult to recover from the same data represented linearly. On these public fixtures, that proposition holds. Residual search can surface candidate leftovers after selection removal; those leftovers remain candidates until independent physics audit.

What this does not claim

Confirmed new fundamental physics. A map of the Universe. That surviving motifs are unnamed discoveries rather than selection effects. That a frontier LLM invented couplings from nothing. That pack-compliant AWARE wins prove a particular commercial model.
