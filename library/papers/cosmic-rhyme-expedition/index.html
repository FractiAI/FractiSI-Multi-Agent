Abstract

This expedition explores protein-folding rhymes on public sky catalogs. E9–E12 showed that a relational awareness board can recover structure a linear pass misses across proteins, knowledge, macro series, and complementary collaboration. Here we ask whether the same grammar — sequence → board → partner — opens NASA, JPL, OpenNGC, and WMAP the same way. Open motifs and residual physics are secondary arms. The residual arm reports two candidates for new physics (WMAP residual lags 11 and 14). Language packs live in the LLM Rhyme expedition.

Primary research question

Does the protein-folding rhyme travel on public cosmic catalogs and spectra the way it traveled on proteins, knowledge, and macroeconomic data?

Experimental rhyme

Protein sequence → couplings → fold. Knowledge documents → source relationships → answer. Macroeconomy series → teleconnections → structure. Cosmos observations → cosmic relationships → ?. The question mark is the discovery target. We do not tell the system to find filaments, voids, or dark-matter maps.

What this does claim

If relational awareness is a general property of intelligence, increasing the relational representation of cosmic data — including as language packs — should expose structure that is difficult to recover from the same data represented linearly. On these public fixtures, that proposition holds. Residual search can surface candidate leftovers after selection removal; those leftovers remain candidates until independent physics audit.

What this does not claim

Confirmed new fundamental physics. A map of the Universe. That surviving motifs are unnamed discoveries rather than selection effects. That a frontier LLM invented couplings from nothing. That pack-compliant AWARE wins prove a particular commercial model.
