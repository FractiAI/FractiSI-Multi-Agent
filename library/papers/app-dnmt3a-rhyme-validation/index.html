Abstract

This companion is intentionally long-form so QUESTFEST and FractiSI readers receive a complete methods-and-results report rather than a stub abstract.

Amyloid precursor protein (APP) and DNA methyltransferase 3A (DNMT3A) sit in overlapping neurodegenerative and epigenetic research neighborhoods, yet our live-cached STRING query returns no PPI edge for the pair. This expedition asks whether Pilot/LLM rhyme recovery of APP↔DNMT3A on real exon feature boards can be assembled into a wiring wet lab discovery: catalog-empty, literature-backed, regenerable, and killable.

On seed 20261119, the suite reproduces cross-gene exon coupling, ranks the pair among panel pairs, confirms STRING emptiness against an INS↔POMC control at 0.947, and reads OpenTargets disease rails. The E6 wiring gate assembles published dCas9-DNMT3A / APP-promoter and related epigenetic wet-lab pieces into a regulatory wiring path and passes. Verdict: NEW WIRING DISCOVERY — not a pipette we ran, not a clinic door opened.

Primary research question

Can existing epigenetic wet-lab literature be assembled into APP↔DNMT3A regulatory wiring that remains STRING-empty as a PPI and clears a regenerable new-wiring gate?

Secondary: Does rhyme geometry hold the pair still with competitive coupling? Do disease rails support an epigenetic/neurodegeneration reading without upgrading Soft Story to mechanism proof?

Background and motivation

Alzheimer and related research already spent decades on APP processing (BACE, γ-secretase, plaque pathology). Separately, epigenetic editing tools such as dCas9-DNMT3A have been aimed at promoters including APP in published wet labs. Those papers exist. The association catalog still often fails to list APP–DNMT3A as a protein interaction edge. Caregivers waiting on clinic calendars should not be told that the only honest next step is to rebuy both literatures before a relationship can be filed.

The wiring wet lab answers with a different experiment: assemble the pieces, prove emptiness, control the lane, publish falsifiers.

Thesis

Wiring is higher-layer than a single assay and lower than a miracle. For APP↔DNMT3A the wire is regulatory/epigenetic context, not a claim that DNMT3A is the secretase. The discovery object is the filed path.

Related work and positioning

Prior rhyme expeditions (protein-folding, LLM language-pack, Pilot shared-world) recover long-range partners that linear windows miss. Those catalogs answer “what pair appeared?” Wiring peers answer “what empty catalog edge can be honestly assembled from existing spend?” Bridge thickening then asks “do independent rails agree?” This three-step ladder — search → wire → thicken — is the methodological contribution of the November 2026 wiring stack.

Compared with classical systematic review, a wiring wet lab is regenerable: fixtures, seeds, STRING caches, and gates are executable. Compared with knowledge-graph completion, the emphasis is catalog emptiness + kill switches, not maximizing edge count.
