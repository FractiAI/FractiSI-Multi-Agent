Abstract

Amyloid precursor protein (APP) and DNA methyltransferase 3A (DNMT3A) sit in overlapping neurodegenerative and epigenetic research neighborhoods, yet our live-cached STRING query returns no PPI edge for the pair. This expedition asks whether Pilot/LLM rhyme recovery of APP↔DNMT3A on real exon feature boards can be assembled into a wiring wet lab discovery: catalog-empty, literature-backed, regenerable, and killable.

On seed 20261119, the suite reproduces cross-gene exon coupling, ranks the pair among panel pairs, confirms STRING emptiness against an INS↔POMC control at 0.947, and reads OpenTargets disease rails. The E6 wiring gate assembles published dCas9-DNMT3A / APP-promoter and related epigenetic wet-lab pieces into a regulatory wiring path and passes. Verdict: NEW WIRING DISCOVERY — not an assay we ran, not a clinic door opened.

Primary research question

Can existing epigenetic wet-lab literature be assembled into APP↔DNMT3A regulatory wiring that remains STRING-empty as a PPI and clears a regenerable new-wiring gate?

Secondary: Does rhyme geometry hold the pair still with competitive coupling? Do disease rails support an epigenetic/neurodegeneration reading without upgrading computational to mechanism proof?

Background and motivation

Alzheimer and related research already spent decades on APP processing (BACE, γ-secretase, plaque pathology). Separately, epigenetic editing tools such as dCas9-DNMT3A have been aimed at promoters including APP in published wet labs. Those papers exist. The association catalog still often fails to list APP–DNMT3A as a protein interaction edge. The only honest next step should not be to repeat both literatures before a relationship can be filed.

The wiring wet lab answers with a different experiment: assemble the pieces, prove emptiness, control the lane, publish falsifiers.

Thesis

Wiring is higher-layer than a single assay and lower than a miracle. For APP↔DNMT3A the wire is regulatory/epigenetic context, not a claim that DNMT3A is the secretase. The discovery object is the filed path.

Qualitative reading

The wire says: published epigenetic tools already touch APP regulation; rhyme boards recover the pair; STRING does not list PPI; therefore a regulatory wiring discovery can be filed without pretending we ran dCas9 ourselves.

Falsifiers and demotion rules

  1. STRING gains high-confidence APP–DNMT3A PPI → rediscovery.
  2. Required epigenetic / promoter wet-lab roles fail citation audit.
  3. Coupling collapses on regenerable panel protocol.
  4. INS↔POMC control fails → stop interpreting emptiness.
  5. Any clinical-efficacy claim appears in the body → editorial demotion (honesty rail only).

Discussion

Wiring wet lab instantiation

APP↔DNMT3A is the epigenetic twin of NPY↔SOD1’s disease-context twin. Same gates, different literature shelf. Together they show the form is not ALS-specific.

Relation to Pilot / LLM rhyme

Pilot catalogs list APP↔DNMT3A among genome exon aware-only partners. This suite converts a catalog hit into a wiring claim with emptiness + assembly.

Limitations

computational · not engine shelf · not treatment · small exon panel · associative OpenTargets · no wet lab performed here · regulatory wiring ≠ binding.

Human stakes without hype

Dementia and epigenetic-editing research already fund overlapping literatures. Filing a killable wire is triage, not a cure. The cost claim is a refusal to demand duplicate spend before the relationship can be inspected.

Related work and positioning

Prior rhyme expeditions (protein-folding, LLM language-pack, Pilot shared-world) recover long-range partners that linear windows miss. Those catalogs answer “what pair appeared?” Wiring peers answer “what empty catalog edge can be honestly assembled from existing spend?” Bridge thickening then asks “do independent rails agree?” This three-step ladder — search → wire → thicken — is the methodological contribution of the November 2026 wiring stack.

Compared with classical systematic review, a wiring wet lab is regenerable: fixtures, seeds, STRING caches, and gates are executable. Compared with knowledge-graph completion, the emphasis is catalog emptiness + kill switches, not maximizing edge count.

Worked example: reproducing the receipt

  3. Read W1–W4 citations.

Extended limitations and non-claims

We do not claim: clinical efficacy; finished particle or CMB laws; that rhyme geometry equals causality; that computational upgrades to engine shelf without a separate pin review; that Vercel deploy state proves scientific validity; that absence of STRING edge equals biological absence.

We do claim: regenerable gates; honest emptiness; citeable assembly; daylight falsifiers; a higher-layer discovery class useful for triage.

Extended experimental narrative

The regenerable receipt is the primary evidence object. Readers should treat tables in this paper as guides to the JSON, not as replacements for it. When seeds, fixtures, or STRING snapshots move, the paper’s locked numbers are historical; the suite’s current run is authoritative for reproduction.

Implications for FractiSI product surfaces

FractiSI Multi-Agent already ships rhyme features that recover partners. Wiring peers teach the product to file relationship objects with kill switches. Bridge thickening teaches the product to park some objects. That is collective intelligence as triage, not as slogan.
