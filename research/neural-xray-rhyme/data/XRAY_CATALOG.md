- **XR-03** Detect — FractiSI searches for recurring organizational relationships.
- **XR-04** Match — Determine whether the same rhyme appears in the other evidence stream.
- **XR-05** Reconstruct — Use the detected rhyme to reconstruct missing or transformed structure.
- **XR-06** Predict — Freeze the rhyme and predict on previously unseen observations.
- **XR-07** Intervene — Alter the identified organizational relationship.
- **XR-08** Observe consequence — Determine whether the predicted system-level change occurs.
