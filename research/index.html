Method

Public receipts gate product claims.

FractiAI publishes open empirical labs. FractiSI repeats only the claims those labs carry.

FractiSI Multi-Agent is the flagship product surface. Tiny Footprint and Tiny Gateway store the shared world once, keep per-agent deltas, and reconstruct selectively. Coordination, composition, Git awareness, and approval-gated deploy sit above them. All of it is documented, versioned, and runnable. None of it claims that a catalog constant replaces laboratory physics.

What the labs and expeditions measured

These runs test discovery and convergence first. Product engine shelves and workstation companions live on the product and library pages, not here.

Wiring Wet Lab. Methodology + combo + peers on real public literature and spectra. Wiring Wet Lab · Bridge thickening · Combo.

Pilot Rhyme expedition. Director holds the map; Pilots run local steps; roles once; mission deltas. 100/100 vs flat 0; 22% smaller than exact role cache. Paper · Blog · Suite.

Rhyming vs non-rhyming expedition. On real protein, GPS and agent messages where the wording changes, rhyming templates stored the traffic 33% to 49% smaller than an exact-match cache that never hit. With mostly identical text, a normal cache still wins. We also ran four open-source world models (DreamerV3, MuZero, TD-MPC2, V-JEPA 2) on a CPU. They are strong for one agent and none can share a world between agents. Paper · Full expedition · Write-up.

Expedition 002 · Beyond Memory. Guided shared-world recovery found 100/100 against 0/100 for a flat skim, with −50% rounds and −85% ops on the same constrained path.
