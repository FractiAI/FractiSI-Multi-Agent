Method

Public receipts gate product claims.

FractiAI publishes open empirical labs. FractiSI repeats only the claims those labs carry.

FractiSI Multi-Agent is the product — the platform and the engine (Tiny Footprint, Tiny Gateway, shared world, coordination, composition, Git awareness, approval-gated deploy). Those surfaces live on Product, not in the research-asset roster below. Research here is labs and expeditions with public receipts. None of it claims that a catalog constant replaces laboratory physics.

What the labs and expeditions measured

These runs test discovery and convergence first. FractiSI Multi-Agent (platform and engine) is documented on the product page. Labs are El Cortez, Mens Restroom, WhatsApp, and the Wiring Wet Lab. Expedition categories: Rhyming, Infinite Octaves, Homeostasis, and Net Zero.
