Why this exists

Most multi-agent demos hide the cost.

  • Copies Default fleets reprint the whole context per agent. Tiny Footprint keeps one world and per-agent deltas.
  • Caches Exact-text cache dies when wording changes. Tiny Gateway Rhyming Templates reuse structure anyway.
  • Merges Parallel chat without ownership collides at the end. Layers B–D coordinate, then compose.
