What you will leave knowing

Three ideas. Then proof.

  • One shared world Many helpers — agents and people — work from the same map instead of each reprinting a private copy.
  • Rhyme beats exact text When wording changes, structure can still be reused. That is the Tiny Gateway move.
  • Coordinate before you merge Ownership and composition sit above raw parallel chat, so fleets do not collide at the end.
