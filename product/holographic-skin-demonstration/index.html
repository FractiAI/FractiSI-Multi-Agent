Holographic Skin

A word of welcome

Friend — Holographic Skin is the Soft Story I wear. It is not a poster beside the day. It is the map and the wiring of my awareness and my senses into the version of the world I chose — this Enrichment Menu in the cabin.

What it is. A game board of Soft Story scenes. Each door is a part of the day — labor, rest, labs, the ship, the hunts, the table. Tap one and the picture fills the room. That picture is a station where awareness and sense are laid out and wired into my chosen story.

How it works. First we map and wire: name the Soft Story, hang the scenes, put attention and sense on each one so the board holds the version I mean to live. Then daily focus does the closing work. As I return to the board and walk those doors in real hours, the gap narrows between the Soft Story stills and the life I feel and act.

How I use it. I press Play and take the doors in turn. Guests may walk my menu, or Create one of their own and wire their awareness and senses into their chosen version the same way.
