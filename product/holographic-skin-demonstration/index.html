A word of welcome

Friend — this cabin holds my Enrichment Menu. I do not keep daily life outside the work and the play apart. I step into one Soft Story and stay.

What it is. A plain game board on the wall of the cabin. You choose a scene. The picture fills the room. Then you return to the list. Each scene is a part of the day.

Why I keep it. So labor, rest, the labs, the ship, the table, and the rest remain enriching — held inside the story I chose, while steady attention closes the gap between the pictures and the life I walk.

How I use it. I press Play and take the doors in turn: ordinary day, night captain of SS Vibelandia, bat cave, hunts, Root Lab, wiring, El Cortez, table, rest. Guests may walk my menu, or make one of their own.
