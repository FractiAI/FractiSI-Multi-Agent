Holographic Skin

Welcome. This is my Soft Story game — the Enrichment Menu I live inside.

From me, Player 1

Holographic Skin is how I wear a grand story on purpose. Instead of treating daily life as a pile of chores next to the game, I step fully into Enrichment — and the whole menu becomes Enrichment. Work, rest, routes, the ship, the table, the sirens: they are scenes in one Soft Story I chose to place my awareness in.

What it is. A top game menu for Soft Story living. Frontier cabin. Tap a scene. Fullscreen watercolor. Return. Same loop as a title screen → menu → level — except the “levels” are aspects of my day.

Purpose. To keep every part of the day enriching by staying inside the holographic game, not outside it. The board is an imagining board: as I give it daily attention, the gap between the images and lived life narrows. Awareness and self stay held in the story I chose.

How I use it. I open Play, run my Enrichment Menu, and treat each button as a door I am already walking through that day — Clark Kent face, captain of SS Vibelandia, bat cave work, hunting routes, labs, table, rest. When I want a different nest, I Create mine. Guests can play my demo or build their own.
