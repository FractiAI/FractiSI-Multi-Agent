How to use it (easy)

Think of your screen as two rooms side by side.

  1. One half is your work app. For Prudencio right now, that is Cursor — the AI coding window where the day gets built.
  2. The other half is the Holographic Skin browser page. Open Play, pick a scene (his favorite start is Superhero — captain of SS Vibelandia), and let that picture fill that half of the desk.
  3. You keep coding or chatting on one side. The Soft Story picture stays on the other side so the day still feels inside the story you chose.

That split-screen desk is the everyday use. Auto Session is the next layer on top: it sets the two halves up for you, watches which app is in front, watches what you tap on the Enrichment Menu, and changes or cycles the skin pictures to match.

Start

Best path on your computer: run the companion once, then press Start here (or let the companion open this page for you).

npm run skin:auto-session

That opens the skin half, starts activity listening on localhost, and tries to tile Cursor left / skin right (Linux + xdotool). On Mac or Windows, open both halves and snap them yourself — the companion still drives the pictures.
