Auto Session

Two halves of your desk. You pick the pictures — or let activity pick them.

Not on phones or tablets yet

Auto Session needs a desktop computer with a split screen (your work app on one half, this skin on the other). Handheld devices are not supported yet.

You can still browse the Enrichment Menu on this device — open Play — but Manual / Autodetect desk session stays on desktop for now.

Open Enrichment Menu

Simple instructions

  1. Split your screen. Work on one half. Skin (browser) on the other.
  2. Other half = any app you choose — not only an IDE. Cursor, Slack, Mail, Chrome, a spreadsheet…
  3. Manual — tap a picture below; it stays until you pick another.
  4. Autodetect — the helper answers what am I doing now? and maps it through your Soft Story:
    • Person in the window (emailing Daniel) → that person’s skin
    • WhatsApp → Root behavior lab · Uber Eats → Duck hunting · Suno / cigar lounge → Restroom behavioral lab · Cursor AI → Frontier work · Other workstation → Bat cave · Sponsor hunt → Tropical marlin
    • Nothing matched → Soft Story mission cycling deck

One-time enable: run npm run skin:auto-session:install once. After that, Autodetect Start launches the helper automatically (and at login).
