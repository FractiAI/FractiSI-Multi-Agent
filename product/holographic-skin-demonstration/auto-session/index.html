Auto Session

Two halves of your desk. You pick the pictures — or let activity pick them.

Simple instructions

  1. Split your screen. Put your work on one half. Put this skin (browser) on the other half.
  2. Other half = any app you choose. It does not have to be an IDE. Cursor, Slack, Mail, a browser tab, a spreadsheet, a game — whatever you are using. (Prudencio often uses Cursor.)
  3. Choose how pictures change:
    • Manual — tap a scene below (or on Play). That picture stays until you pick another.
    • Autodetect — run the small helper on your computer, then leave this on Autodetect. When you switch apps, the skin picks (or cycles) pictures from your rules.

Picture mode

Manual is on. Tap a picture below to put it on the skin half.

Pick a picture (Manual)

Tap any scene. It opens full on the skin half (or updates the open skin window).

Autodetect setup

One time on this computer, start the helper. It watches which app is in front (any app — not only coding tools) and drives the skin pictures from your rules.

npm run skin:auto-session

Optional flags: --other "Slack" · --scene superhero · --port 18765. On Mac/Windows, snap the two windows yourself; the helper still changes pictures.

Autodetect waits until the helper is running.
