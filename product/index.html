What the product pays for

FractiSI Multi-Agent is the product name; Tiny Footprint is the shared-world execution core. Labs show four payoffs together: shared-world discovery that flat skim misses, faster convergence on the same path, density after gzip when every agent would otherwise carry a full copy, and rhyme reuse when wording differs but shape matches. Memory concurrency is one meter—not the whole story. Network fan-out and durable template stores are phase-2 adapters—not separate diluted SKUs.
