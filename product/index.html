How it works

Shared world

Policies, maps, tools, corpora, and simulation state are registered once as a shared template.

Per-agent deltas

Pose, goals, inventory, and local memory. Only what differs from the shared world.

Selective reconstruction

Nested vectors let an agent load only the scales it needs. The saving applies to the runtime working set, not only file size.

Honest limits

  • Lab figures are FractiSI architectural and structured-state measurements. They inform product decisions. They are not CapEx guarantees.
  • The stack runs as catalog and in-process labs inside Syntheverse Sandbox, on Base Mainnet as bedrock. Entering and leaving is voluntary. No crypto purchase is required.
  • Net Zero here means a balanced zero under continuous activity. It is not empty void, not climate-policy net zero, and not certified zero-watt computing.
  • Rhyme is not a prefix cache. ΦEGS and Infinite Octaves are design grammar for nesting, not physics and not measured infinite tiers.
  • Tiny Gateway (engine shelf #52) is not a production HBM proof. The hexagon–circle question is partly open.
  • This is not a Theory of Everything, not an Active Inference claim, and awareness here is not consciousness.
