The idea

What goes wrong with a crowd of agents

  • Copies: ten agents, ten copies of one world
  • Context tax: every copy fills memory
  • Different words: exact-text caches go cold when agents paraphrase
  • Buried answers: a skim misses what sits several links away
  • Collisions: many writers step on each other · we come back to this at the stack

Keep one world. Let each agent keep only what makes it different.

Two words you need

  • Net Zero: balanced zero, not nothing zero · forces cancel, work continues
