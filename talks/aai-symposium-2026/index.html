The idea

What goes wrong with many agents.

  • Copies: ten agents, ten copies of one world
  • Memory: every copy fills it
  • Paraphrase: exact-text caches go cold when agents reword
  • Discovery and convergence: a skim misses answers several links away
  • Collisions: agents overwrite each other · revisited at the stack

Keep one world. Each agent stores only what differs.

On track /test-drive/ beat 4

The idea

Net Zero, then rhyme.

  • Net Zero: balanced zero, not nothing zero · forces cancel and work continues · octave 0
  • Rhyme: organized alike even when the words differ
  • Four pillars, all required, rooted at Net Zero: repeating · self-similar · self-correcting · recursive
  • A pattern that passes becomes a Rhyming Template and is reused. Matching words or a cache hit do not qualify.

Balanced zero, not nothing zero.
