# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or hardware budget.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and respectful when discussing code, measurements, or claims.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another participant's contact details, receipts, or share packets.
- Ask before sharing benchmark data outside the repository.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately misrepresenting measurements, hardware, or evidence.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or validation discussions.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to `valetpru@gmail.com`. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
