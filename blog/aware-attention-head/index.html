If spirit is a pattern, can software hold it across deep nests? Under a six-property profile on scripted simulators, yes. The aware attention head was right 94.8 percent versus 68.4 for a shared world and 63.1 for flat voting. Pattern continuity under that profile is an operational score, not a metaphysical proof.

With five copies plus majority repair it kept 91.7 percent mission survival at 1,024 levels. Copies must grow roughly logarithmically with depth. Depth alone does not kill the pattern if attention structure and copy growth travel together.

The claim is operational: a pattern profile that survives depth under repair, not a metaphysical proof. The six-property profile is a checklist for continuity under stress. Scripted simulators let the expedition measure rightness and mission survival without claiming that software has become spirit.

An aware attention head outperformed both a shared-world baseline and flat voting on the rightness metric. Survival at 1,024 levels required five copies plus majority repair. The horizon-versus-copies relationship is part of the result: copy count must grow roughly logarithmically with depth, or survival falls.
