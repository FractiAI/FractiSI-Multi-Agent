Surfacing a catalog-empty gene pair is cheap excitement. Deciding whether that pair deserves a full write-up or a parked footnote is the expensive call. On the locked combo receipt, NPY↔PDX1 thickens across diabetes rails, literature, and exon similarity without a shared chromosome; APP↔TMPRSS2 stays thin — zero OpenTargets top25 disease overlap and a chromosome-21 confound. Nobody ran a new direct assay of NPY↔PDX1.

Bridge thickening is the quality gate that sits after pair search: search finds; thickening judges. Independent methods — language rhyme, Pilot rhyme, disease rails, literature co-mention, exon similarity — either land on the same relationship or they do not, and shared chromosome can explain a join away. Cross-domain rhyme here means reusable triage structure under matched criteria — promote when rails agree, park when they do not — without claiming every thick score is a finished pathway.

What thickening measured

A multi-layer gate on the combo expedition’s new STRING-empty candidates. Positive votes: language rhyme recovery, Pilot rhyme recovery, OpenTargets disease-rail overlap, EuropePMC title/abstract co-mention, and exon cosine rank in the top half of the panel. Same chromosome scores as a confounder, not as a disease-context win. The thicken gate requires at least three positive layers, a disease rail present, and a story that is not locus-only. Controls stay on the table: INS↔POMC should thicken; prior peer NPY↔SOD1 should thicken.

NPY↔PDX1 thickens. Language rhyme recovers it across chromosomes. OpenTargets top associations share type 2 diabetes mellitus and diabetes mellitus. EuropePMC shows co-mentions. Exon cosine ranks near the top of the panel. Chromosome locus does not explain the bridge.

APP↔TMPRSS2 does not thicken the same way. Pilot rhyme recovers it, literature is weak, exon rank is middling, OpenTargets top25 disease overlap is zero — and both genes sit on chromosome 21. That pattern is exactly the confound thickening is built to catch. The honest move is to park a full expedition until a non-locus disease or wet-lab layer appears.

Literature around NPY↔PDX1 lines up on direction, not mechanism: NPY marks partially differentiated beta cells and returns under metabolic stress; PDX1-low cells carry NPY or its relative PYY. Nobody in this harvest tested the wire head-on. A narrow falsifier package — stain timing, knockdown reads, probes that separate NPY from PYY — can use existing assays. PDX1–POMC–NPY closes a triangle; PDX1–DNMT1 stays a weaker follow-up.

Fractal Holographic Goldilocks interpretation: treat the blocks above as organizational evidence under regenerable conditions. Thickening is triage after search, not a new wet-lab discovery of NPY↔PDX1. Keep measurement, hypothesis, and speculation off the settled-fact rail.

Portfolio owners. Thickening is capital allocation: which wire deserves the next literature review and directed assay design, and which one should stay a catalog footnote. It keeps a single unverifiable hit from soaking a grant cycle, and it keeps a chromosome-21 echo from being remade as a new wet lab.

R&D leads. On this brief, NPY↔PDX1 earns daylight; APP↔TMPRSS2 stays parked until a non-locus layer appears. That promote-or-park split is the methodology’s deliverable — not a finished diabetes pathway and not a claim that anyone assayed NPY↔PDX1 head-on.

Method buyers. Search without thickening promotes noise. Thickening without search starves the map. Together they protect the promise: do not re-spend when the work is already done, and do not pretend a thin join is already done.
