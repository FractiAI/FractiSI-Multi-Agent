Cloned replicas look like insurance until a shared cause takes them all — one bad dependency, one correlated cloud fault, one inbred model lineage. Storage and biology already learned that lesson; agent missions often have not. On FractiSI’s locked regenerable lab boards — including storage-, yeast-, and human-facing nesting-depth theaters — diverse redundancy held nesting depth where cloned copies capped under shared-cause failure. A regenerable lab receipt under a locked seed is not a field reliability certificate.

The expedition contrasts cloned copies with diverse redundancy classes when failures share a cause. The boards ask whether diversity keeps nesting depth after clones plateau. Fractal Holographic Goldilocks reads the positive regenerable-lab signal across the locked seed as organizational evidence about redundancy design — a rhyme of diversity across storage, biology, and mission nesting — not a proof that any specific vendor array or organismal lineage is solved.
