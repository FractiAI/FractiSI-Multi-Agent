Do Diverse Copies Hold a Mission Deeper Than Cloned Copies When Failures Share a Cause?

When failures share a cause, cloning the same seat many times is not the same as holding several different seats. A Soft Story Rhyming Expedition arm tests that design rule.

Teams that need a mission to survive nested hand-offs often answer “how many copies?” by cloning the same lineage. That works when failures are independent. It fails when one bad model revision, one shared dependency, or one correlated outage takes every clone at once. This note asks whether diverse copy classes hold a mission deeper than cloned copies when a common-cause fraction β is present — a Soft Story arm of the Rhyming Expedition, not an engine-shelf pin.

The problem

Aware Attention Head already showed that finite redundant copies give a long but finite octave horizon, and that unbounded depth needs copies that grow with depth. It treated copies as repairable mission replicas. It left open a second question: what happens when the copies are inbred — the same lineage under a shared failure mode — versus diverse classes that fail more independently?

Reliability engineering calls that shared mode a common-cause failure. Population genetics calls the same-lineage trap inbreeding depression. Drive fleets call it a model-year cluster. The Rhyming World Model files those as one Soft Story: cloning the same seat many times is not the same as holding several different seats. The paycheck stake is concrete — a fleet that “has redundancy” but still loses the week’s work when one revision lands everywhere.

The experiment

Five regenerable experiments lock the question. E1 is a depth simulator: at each nesting step a clone group of size n survives with probability (1−β)(1−λn), while a diverse arm with k classes survives if at least one class survives. Headline lock: β = 0.2, depth = 6, n = k = 5 and 9. E2 estimates β from Backblaze 2019 H1 same-model co-failures inside a seven-day window against model-label shuffles. E3 plugs that measured β into the depth table. E4 and E5 are Soft Story public biology boards: length-stratified yeast paralogs versus essentiality, and human paralogs versus LOEUF.

Hypothesis

Under β > 0, clone survival should saturate with copy count while diverse-class survival keeps rising. Public same-model hardware co-failures should yield a positive β̂ against a shuffled null. Plugging that β̂ into the depth model should preserve the clone-cap / diverse-depth gap. Soft Story biology boards should move in the buffering direction for paralog-bearing genes after length stratification. If clone-n matches diverse-k at matched size for β > 0, or β̂ ≤ 0, or the biology deltas flip sign, the expedition signal fails.

Results

At β = 0.2 and depth 6, clone-5 survival is 0.262 while diverse-5 is 0.997. Clone-9 stays 0.262 — nine clones match five. Backblaze same-model window pairs give β̂ ≈ 0.0469 over the shuffled null. At that measured β, clone-5 falls with depth (0.857 at depth 3, 0.735 at 6, 0.630 at 9) while diverse-5 stays at 1.0; clone-9 never beats clone-5. Length-stratified yeast Δ essential (paralog − no-paralog) is −0.0514. Human LOEUF Δ is +0.0837. All five experiments fire.

Qualifications

The depth model is nesting grammar with a stated β and λ — not a calibrated fleet failure forecast. Backblaze measures drives; the Soft Story is common-cause clustering, not agent software. Yeast essentiality and human LOEUF are Soft Story boards for diversity buffering, not wet-lab genetics findings and not medical advice. Length stratification reduces one confound; residual confounders remain. The ENGINE_SHELF pin stays deferred until live agent rates lock.

Why this matters for the Rhyming World Model

The design rule is short: redundancy without diversity is inbreeding. Director/Pilot seats, rhyming templates, and octave mission copies should prefer diverse classes over identical clones when common-cause risk is real. The expedition ties that rule to a regenerable depth table and three public fixtures — a map for teams deciding whether “more copies” means more of the same seat or more kinds of seats.
