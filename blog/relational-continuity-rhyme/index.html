Short answer: Yes. Rhyme accuracy 1.0 across partners, QA, agent worlds, and seed↔edge; exact cache 0; lexical similarity ≤0.14. Removing rhyme dropped partners to 0.083.

What we measured

One relational-rhyme operation across partners, question↔answer gaps, agent-world complements, and seed↔edge after octave transforms that strip inherited seed text. Arms: exact cache, token-Jaccard similarity, shared bag without index, FractiSI minus rhyme, FractiSI rhyme.

Results

  • Partners: rhyme 1.0 vs embedding ~0.14
  • QA: rhyme 1.0; lexical trap wins 0/120
  • Seed↔edge (depth 8): rhyme 1.0 without inherited seed text
  • Ablation: minus rhyme drops partners to 0.083
