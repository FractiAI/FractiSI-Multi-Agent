What kind of wet lab this is

A physical wet lab invents a measurement. A catalog scores an edge. A wiring wet lab does neither from scratch. It asks whether the relationship you need can be filed from work that already exists — with named kill switches — so an existing relationship can be filed without repeating finished assays. That is not softer biology. It is a higher-layer experiment whose virtue is refusing duplicate cost when the shelf is already full.

The experiment that defines the form

Gates any wiring wet lab must pass: rhyme coupling recovers the pair; the association catalog stays empty (or provisional with named kills); a positive control proves the catalog lane works; existing empirical pieces fill pre-registered roles; kill conditions are published before celebration. FractiSI peers instantiate the form on real public data — ALS wet labs wiring NPY to SOD1, DNMT3A→APP wet labs wiring epigenetic regulation, public microwave spectra wiring residual multipoles, and a combo of protein-folding, language, and Pilot rhymes surfacing further empty candidates such as NPY↔PDX1.

None of those peers claim FractiSI ran the original assays. They claim the wiring wet lab ran: join, cite, control, kill.

Hypothesis

If the wiring wet lab is real, “already done” should be the first experiment, not the last excuse. Gates should pass when existing pieces assemble into a catalog-empty edge, and fail when STRING fills, citations collapse, or spectrum falsifiers kill a residual wire — without demanding a second grant to re-measure what is already on the shelf.

What we saw

The form is operational. NPY↔SOD1 and APP↔DNMT3A clear STRING-empty wiring gates using published wet labs. WMAP lags clear a provisional spectrum wire with rediscovery controls and a beam/Planck kill board. The combo expedition surfaces new empty candidates without a new physical assay spend for those joins. Regenerable JSON replaces slogans. The gain is the same everywhere: inspectable maps that do not require repeating completed work before a next step exists.

How kill switches keep the form honest

A wiring claim dies if the catalog grows the PPI edge — that is rediscovery, not failure of science. It dies if the required wet-lab roles cannot be cited. Spectrum wiring dies if Planck stays silent or beam-injected simulations recreate the lag. A discovery form without kill switches is just marketing with footnotes. Director and Pilot seats arguing over one FractiSI map make missing controls harder to hide. AI can re-run the receipt after every catalog refresh. Demotion stays cheap; duplicate assays stay optional.

Why reuse is a capital decision

Duplicate assays burn grant money and years while answers wait in other folders. A wiring wet lab does not open the clinic door by itself — honesty stays at the end of this piece for a reason — but it stops the false requirement that every relationship start with a new invoice. When a wire holds, the next physical lab can target the thinnest missing link. When a kill switch fires, demote and move on. For executives funding FractiSI triage stacks, that is the FGC read: pay for assembly and falsifiers first; pay for new wet assays only where the map still has a hole.
