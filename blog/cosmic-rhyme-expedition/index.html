How the search was built

C1–C3 were never the physics claim. They showed the protein-folding LINEAR / FLAT / AWARE grammar recovers long-range partners on NASA exoplanets, JPL close approaches, OpenNGC, and WMAP TT (~0% → 100%), and that open motif search mostly finds catalog fingerprints (survey history, listed size ↔ sky/brightness). Useful for anyone who has cleaned a cross-agency table. Not a new landlord for the universe — which is why we added residual search.
