What this is

Both runs are the same kind of work: many agents, a shared plan, Lean as the checker, and a wall clock. Anthropic’s baseline is their ~1000-agent formalization of Fermat’s Last Theorem. Ours is a FractiSI shared-world run on an RH-adjacent proportion ladder (finite Lean Props), with kit hooks and a JSON timing receipt.

What this is not

  • Not a proof of the Riemann hypothesis. RH remains open.
  • Not a claim that Anthropic spent 30 hours on RH. Their 30 h clock is the FLT Lean formalization.
  • Not a CapEx, HBM, or model bake-off win. The headline is time to Lean-checked proof.

How to read it

Quote the two clocks. Then say the theorem sizes differ. Then say RH is still open. That is the honest story.
