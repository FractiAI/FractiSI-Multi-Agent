Did FractiSI close Anthropic’s Riemann invent challenge? No. Challenge headlines sit at 0 of 17. A separate paper-math invent ladder closed 4 of 4. The Riemann Hypothesis remains open.

That split is the story. Ladder progress is real under paper-math rules. Challenge closure is not. Any marketing that converts a ladder win into a Challenge win is selling a different result than the one on record.

Honesty-first math campaigns are rare because the incentives run the other way. A team that invents under Challenge rules, reports what closed and what did not, and refuses to swap labels is choosing a slower headline. FractiSI’s Riemann expedition takes that slower path on purpose.

Two lanes matter. One lane invents under Anthropic-comparable Challenge headlines and remains at 0 of 17. Another lane invents under paper-math rules and closes a 4 of 4 ladder. Readers who blur those lanes will cite the wrong victory.

The Riemann Hypothesis itself sits outside both lanes as an open problem. Closing a finite invent ladder is not the same act as sealing Challenge Solutions, and neither act settles RH. The expedition’s value is the refusal to pretend otherwise.
