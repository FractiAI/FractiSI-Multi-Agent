Do Rhyming World Models Outperform Non-Rhyming Ones?

Published
November 18, 2026
By
FractiAI Research
Reading time

Read the paper Head-to-head Final report

Short answer: Yes. Against exact-text caches on real non-identical observations (PDB and Movebank data); on task success it only matched a shared structured blackboard, and prefix reuse still won when strings were identical. Genie, Cosmos and V-JEPA were not executable, so no scores were reported for them.

Fair comparative harness. Shared worlds beat independent agents on collective knowledge, planning, and relational tasks. FractiSI-shaped arms tied a competent shared structured blackboard. Dreamer-family, TD-MPC-lite, and MuZero-lite ran live on CPU. Genie remains closed; V-JEPA/Cosmos need GPU not on this VM.

Verdicts

  • Shared world as coordination primitive: PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED
  • FractiSI rhyme vs non-rhyming exact/prefix: PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED (beats exact-cache on real non-identical text; loses to prefix when strings are identical)
  • FractiSI vs shared_structured on task: parity on E9 (both 1.0; independent 0)
  • Evidence level: Level 2 computational fixtures — not Level 5 embodied

What we measured

Worlds A–D plus relational. Ablations: independent · shared text · shared structured · FractiSI · +deltas · +selective reconstruction. Scaling N=1…32. Exp 002 reproduction: 100/100 vs 0/100 guided vs flat.

Where we were wrong

v1 under-tested rhyme. v2 shows rhyme beating exact-text caches on real E9 and Movebank utterances when organization rhymes but wording differs — while prefix reuse still wins on identical system strings. Shared structured state remains enough for task merge; FractiSI’s distinctive edge is organizational reuse, not a monopoly on sharing.

Application companion computational. Do not assume FractiSI wins. Live open WM arms executed; only Genie/V-JEPA/Cosmos remain unavailable here. WP-RHYMING-VS-NON-RHYMING-WORLD-MODELS-EGS-2026-11-18.
