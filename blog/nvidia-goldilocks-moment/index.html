Does NVIDIA need a new world model more than a new CEO. As an organizational case, Yes. Under matched budgets, private silos scored 0 of 100 on a six-step chain while a shared world scored 100 of 100. On sixteen public protein tasks, the same model recovered the partner on 0 of 16 from a linear sequence and 10 of 16 from an explicit relational map. This is not succession advice.

A closed plan-and-role stack works when jobs stay stable. A shared rhyming world model organizes relationships so capable agents stay useful without reprinting private copies. NVIDIA shapes the environment those agents run in; Jensen Huang is the visible leadership case. The lab numbers are evidence about representation — not a call to swap a CEO.
