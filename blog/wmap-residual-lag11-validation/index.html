Provisional means the signal clears today’s filters and can still die under kill tests. Residual lag wiring asks whether structure remains after a smooth continuum is removed from WMAP multipole residuals. Lags 11 and 14 are the reported peaks in this filing.

Shuffle tests put the observed correlations against a null near p of 0.003. That is enough to file the lags as provisional wiring candidates. It is not enough to retire beam systematics. Cosmology buyers and AI teams that reuse sky panels both need that distinction.
