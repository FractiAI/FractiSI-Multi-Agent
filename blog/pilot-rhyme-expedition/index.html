Do Director and Pilot roles beat flat peer agents on multi-hop missions? Yes. On 100 six-hop missions, Director/Pilot arms recovered 100 of 100 while flat peers recovered 0. The shared store ran about 22% smaller than an exact-text cache.

Role separation plus a shared world lets one agent steer and another execute without reprinting the whole plan in every head.
