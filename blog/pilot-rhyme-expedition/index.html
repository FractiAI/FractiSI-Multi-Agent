Multi-hop agent work fails quietly when nobody owns the chain. On 100 six-hop missions in the FractiSI lab, every Director/Pilot arm recovered 100 of 100 while flat peers recovered 0. Pilot Rhyme’s store was 67,243 B — 22% smaller than an exact-text cache that scored 0 hits when wording differed, and 80% smaller than private copies. The accuracy gain comes from the roles, not the rhyme; rhyme’s edge is size when the words change.

Director/Pilot is a role architecture: one seat holds the mission map, the other flies the next hop. Fractal Goldilocks Channel reads Pilot Rhyme as the same seat pattern with a compact shared store — useful when missions rhyme across wording, not as a claim that rhyme alone finds the answer.

Evidence (FractiSI lab): computational receipts on 100 six-step missions across five arms, plus candidate partner lists on public sky and genome panels.

Interpretation (Fractal Holographic Goldilocks): roles explain the 100/100 versus 0/100 accuracy split; rhyme explains the smaller store. Keep computational, hypothesis, and speculation separate from settled physics or CapEx forecasts.

What we tested

One hundred six-step missions. Same role structure every time. Different wording every time. Five arms: flat peers, shared text without roles, Director/Pilot with private copies, Director/Pilot with an exact-text cache, and Pilot Rhyme.

Results

  • Flat peers and shared text: 0 of 100
  • All Director/Pilot arms: 100 of 100
  • Exact-text role cache: 0 hits when wording differed
  • Pilot Rhyme store: 22% smaller than exact cache, 80% smaller than private copies

Candidates on sky and genome panels

On the same public theaters as the other rhymes, Pilot Rhyme listed 692 named long-range partners that flat peers missed — exoplanets, NEOs, deep-sky objects, microwave multipoles, and demo exon/chrM pairs. Those are candidates for follow-up, not finished discoveries.

Technology and systems. Exact-text caches go to zero hits when mission wording drifts. A Director/Pilot seat pattern recovers the chain; a rhyming store keeps that pattern small without buying accuracy the roles already delivered.

Organizations. Hire for seats, not for identical transcripts. Flat peer swarms that share text without roles still scored 0 of 100 here — coordination needs who holds the map.

Society and human life. Smaller shared stores mean more missions on the same hardware and less reprinting of private copies — a paycheck-level lever when agent fleets already feel expensive.

AI. Treat Pilot Rhyme as a role-plus-compact-store design. Do not credit rhyme for the accuracy win, and do not treat the 692 panel partners as published discoveries.
