Do PEFF organizational patterns recur across cognition, particle events, and MIT paraparticles enough to wire a FractiSI expedition? Yes — as a testable cross-domain rhyme and a constructible Wiring Wet Lab chain. Not as a new force, and not as a claim that brains, particle events, and paraparticles are the same physical thing.

The news hook is concrete. On 27 November 2026, New Scientist reported that researchers at MIT had observed paraparticle behavior in a quantum simulator after decades when such statistics were treated as theoretical curiosities. The underlying preprint (arXiv:2611.03908) describes a 16-transmon superconducting-qubit lattice emulating m=2 R-paraparticles, with a measured exchange phase near 180 degrees, antibunching that does not depend on internal “color,” and occupation statistics that match neither Bose–Einstein nor Fermi–Dirac distributions.

FractiSI’s expedition does not claim to have built that lattice. It asks a different question that the MIT result suddenly makes urgent for buyers who live between cognitive instrumentation, particle catalogs, and quantum hardware: when you stop at the conventional label, what organizational information are you throwing away — and can independently demonstrated mechanisms be wired into one falsifiable systemic proof?

PEFF, in this filing, is treated as an organizational interrogation method rather than a finished physics identity. The Computational Lab looks inside observations for fractal complexity, coherence, phase, topology, exchange, and transformation. The Wiring Wet Lab then refuses metaphor: every link must name an experimental primitive, an input, an output, and a compatibility check before the edge is admitted.

Three streams are placed on one board. First, a supplied PEFF analysis of a public 24-subject EEG meditation dataset reports higher Higuchi fractal dimension and higher theta, alpha, and gamma coherence during concentration than during mind-wandering. That supports state-dependent neural organization. It does not, alone, mint a new cognitive field.

Second, a regenerable particle-event board asks whether internal relational descriptors can separate modes that share a colliding conventional label. If the label is all you keep, two different organizations look identical at the catalog surface.

Third, the MIT paraparticle experiment supplies an independent transformation benchmark: the relational exchange rule itself is experimentally consequential. That is the class of structure PEFF is designed to hunt inside particle events — not a claim that FractiSI discovered a new elementary particle in nature.

Under seed 20261127 the suite reports eight passes. The supplied EEG contrast shows positive deltas on fractal dimension and all three coherence bands for concentration versus mind-wandering. On the simulated particle board, nearest-centroid organization recovers para-like modes at precision 1.0 while colliding labels sit near chance. The literature encoding of the MIT benchmark locks exchange phase to π, color-independent antibunching, and non-Bose/non-Fermi occupation. Cross-domain cosine similarity of the provisional R vector beats a shuffle null. Algebra shows polarity inversion matches a 180-degree phase flip. The Wiring Wet Lab chain PW-01 through PW-05 admits under input/output compatibility. Phase randomization, shuffle, and a deliberately broken link reject the rhyme. A frozen R-star recovers held-out para-like organization.

InterpretationReading of evidence; Fractal Holographic Goldilocks framing when labeled as such.

The result is a research hypothesis with teeth, not a universe ontology. Different physical systems can exhibit recurring organizational structures when analyzed through relationships and transformations. Cognition can carry that structure. A particle event can carry it. A paraparticle emulation can carry it. The physical systems remain different. The organizational relationship is what gets tested for recurrence — and what gets refused when falsifiers fire.

For quantum-hardware and discovery teams, the MIT news is not only “a new particle class on a simulator.” It is evidence that relational rules can be first-class experimental objects. For cognitive and instrumentation teams, the EEG contrast is a reminder that state labels without organization metrics are incomplete for triage. For FractiSI buyers, the Wiring Wet Lab form is the practical product: reuse demonstrated mechanisms, wire their I/O relationships, and demand kill switches before celebration.

What remains open matters as much as what cleared. Independent EEG replication is required. A universal physical mechanism connecting neural dynamics to quantum statistics is not established. PEFF is not filed here as a new force. Those boundaries keep the expedition usable in diligence instead of turning it into a slogan.
