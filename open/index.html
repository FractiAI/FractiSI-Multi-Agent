Open source

Why we keep the products open.

If strangers cannot run it, refute it, or fork it, it is not a claim we will stand on.

FractiSI ships Tiny Footprint—memory software that sits beside the AI you already run. Behind it sits the FractiAI research spine: papers, empirical suites, and a public Workstation kit. We keep that spine open on purpose. Not as decoration. As the way we tell the truth.

Who we are

FractiAI is the house that publishes. FractiSI is the commercial vehicle that turns the latest lab into a product a developer can install. Same vow on both sides: preserve the signal, refine the pattern, navigate the unknown. Fair exchange. A claim only after the pipeline has been run.

We are not building a closed cathedral of credentials. The onboarding file, the papers, and the kit exist so another edge—another machine, another team, another skeptic—can get up to speed without asking us for a password to the truth.

Why open matters for superintelligence work

Work that aims at more capable systems attracts secrecy. We take the opposite bet. Closed labs can move fast and still be wrong for years. Open publication with runnable receipts is slower on the press release and faster on correction. Credentials are optional. Code execution is mandatory.

That stance is anti-gatekeeping, not anti-rigor. Soft story stays labeled soft story. Lab numbers stay lab numbers. We do not flatten narrative grammar into a “everything is proven” claim so that openness becomes advertising. Openness without honesty is just louder fog.

The defiant line we keep is short: run it · refute it · fork it.

What staying open actually protects

Falsifiability. A stranger with a laptop should be able to clone the Workstation, run the comparison, and disagree with us using the same instruments. If only we can reproduce the result, we do not get to sell it as settled.

Cloneable edges. Lite edges and public pipes mean repositories and deployments can be copied without a secrets warehouse at the center. Center verifies and routes. It does not warehouse guest life.

Anti-hoarding. Scarcity culture treats knowledge as inventory. We treat signal as something to preserve in the open so the pattern can be refined by more than one desk. Structural harmony over corporate hoarding is not a slogan we invented for this page—it is how the house already publishes.

What is free, and what is not

Open does not mean “everything forever for free.” Community Workstation is free. The repositories and supporting papers are public. Developer Pro, Server, and Cloud are how FractiSI pays the people who keep the product alive. You can verify without buying. You buy when the software earns a place beside your fleet.

Fair exchange stays available: honor rails, human door, tip when clarity landed, ignore the tip jar when you only needed a receipt. Paid tiers are proposed terms, not a hostage situation.

How to verify

Clone tiny-footprint-workstation. Open it in your editor. Run doctor → install → demo → benchmark → report. Read the whitepaper in the library. You should get a reproducible comparison without FractiSI operating your box.
