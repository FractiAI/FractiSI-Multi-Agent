Open source

Why we stay open source

Anyone can clone the kit, run the comparison, and disagree with us on the same instruments. Verifying a claim should not require buying anything.

Open is the safer path

As these systems get more capable, the cost of an uncaught error rises. Secrecy can protect a lead for a quarter. It cannot protect against compounding error. If people outside the team cannot run, check, and correct the work, the team stays wrong longer than it can afford.

That is risk management, not branding.

Integration over exclusion

Frontier discussion often frames the work as a race to close the weights, close the stack, and win the moat. We take the opposite position. The value of more capable systems is in integrating more of the world's work, its verification, and its correction, into systems that stay accountable.

Integration needs interfaces that outsiders can touch. A closed stack integrates only what its owner allows. That is the posture behind FractiSI Multi-Agent and the research that feeds it.

Open does not mean always free

Open means public repositories, published papers, and a Community Workstation anyone can clone. It does not mean every edition is free forever.

Community is free so verification stays free. Developer Pro, Server, and Cloud exist so the people who maintain the product can keep doing it. Verify without buying. Buy when the software earns a place beside your fleet.

Falsifiability is the floor

A skeptic with a laptop should be able to run the comparison we publish. If only we can reproduce a result, it is not ready to sell.

How to verify

Clone FractiSI-Multi-Agent. Provide Git and a Vercel cloud token, then run node scripts/tiny.mjs try for a live preview. Or run doctor, demo, benchmark, and report locally without a token. Read the paper in the library. Join open evaluation if you want partner measurement. The comparison is reproducible without FractiSI operating your machine.
