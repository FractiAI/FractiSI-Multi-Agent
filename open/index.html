Open source

Why we stay open

If a stranger cannot run it, refute it, or fork it, the claim is not settled.

Closed stacks can stay wrong longer

Frontier work attracts secrecy. We take the other bet. A closed lab can move fast and still be wrong for years. Public code and runnable receipts are slower on the launch email and faster on correction. That is why Tiny Footprint and its research stay open.

Falsifiability is the bar

A skeptic with a laptop should clone the Workstation, run the same comparison, and disagree with us on the same instruments. If only we can reproduce the result, it is not ready to sell as settled. Openness without that bar is marketing.

Free to verify · paid when it earns the seat

Community Workstation is free. Repositories and papers are public. Developer Pro, Server, and Cloud fund the people who keep the product alive. Verify without buying. Buy when the software earns a place beside your fleet.

How to verify

Clone tiny-footprint-workstation. Run doctor → install → demo → benchmark → report. Read the paper in the library. Reproducible comparison—without FractiSI operating your box.
