Open role

Founding CEO

Memory and accelerators have never been tighter. That is when a product like Tiny Footprint becomes the commercial fight—not a side project.

The market case

Every serious AI operator is fighting the same shortages: HBM and DRAM constrained, GPU hours expensive and contended, another rack slow and political. A large share of what those boxes hold is duplicate work—the same shared context reprinted per session, intermediate state kept independently when it could live once.

Tiny Footprint installs beside the stack the customer already runs. No rip-and-replace. Attack the waste: shared pattern once, difference per session, reconstruct on demand. More concurrent work on infrastructure already paid for.

When capacity is loose, memory-efficiency software is optional. When capacity is tight, it is how you stretch the fleet you cannot expand fast enough. That is the window.

Why equity is the point

Frontier AI valuations assume infrastructure remains the bottleneck—who serves more model per watt and per GB, who packs more sessions into the same box, who owns the layer next to inference.

This is an early commercial seat on that layer. Not a finished unicorn package. Founding equity—often mid-teens to low thirties for a true external CEO at this stage—while the product is real, the research is public, and the pressure is already here. Cash starts modest or deferred until revenue or a raise. The upside is ownership timed against that scarcity.

If that trade does not move you, this role will not either.

The seat

Founding CEO—or Co-founder / CEO. Technical founders stay on invention. You build the company: go-to-market, operations, fundraising, first hires, first pilots. COO / Head of GTM works if the titles match how you operate—so long as you have sold and scaled under real infra pressure. We are not hiring a Chief Scientist as first executive. That spine is staffed. See Company.

First ninety days: tighten entity and ops, land credits and design partners, move one real pilot, put raise or revenue on the calendar.

Who fits

People who shipped or sold where GPU cloud, inference platforms, or enterprise AI ops made memory and latency the argument—not a slide. Operators from Lambda, Runpod, CoreWeave, Together, Fireworks, Anyscale; NVIDIA or AMD partner / solutions people who left for a company. Warm introductions beat spray.

What we already have

Public Workstation. Public research spine. Local lab results and an H100 simulation path on the rhyme case prefix caches miss. Live partner silicon is next. Serious candidates get a short pack: product note, validation plan, target list.
