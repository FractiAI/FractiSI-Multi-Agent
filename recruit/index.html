Open role

Founding CEO

We are looking for a founding CEO to build the company around FractiSI Multi-Agent. The product and the public research exist. The commercial seat does not.

Mission

Make shared-world infrastructure the standard way agent fleets run. FractiSI Multi-Agent stores the shared context once and keeps only per-agent differences. It installs beside the agent runtime a customer already uses.

Why now

Operators are short on HBM and DRAM. GPU hours are expensive and contended. Adding a rack is slow. Multi-agent systems make this worse, because policies, maps, and contexts get duplicated per worker. Fleets also miss discoveries that flat skims never assemble, and they collide at merge time.

When capacity is loose, memory-efficiency software is optional. When capacity is tight and agents multiply, it determines how far an existing fleet stretches. DRAM is the engine. FractiSI Multi-Agent is the product. Tiny Footprint is the core that customers feel.

What you will own

Everything commercial. Go-to-market, operations, fundraising, first hires, and first pilots with agent platforms and inference hosts. The technical founders stay on invention, architecture, and proof. See Company.

The first ninety days: tighten the entity and operations, land credits and design partners, move one real multi-agent pilot, and put a raise or revenue on the calendar.

What exists

A public Workstation. The Multi-Agent paper and its empirical suite. Local lab results. Live partner silicon is the next step. Serious candidates receive a short pack: product note, validation plan, and target list.

What we need

Someone who has shipped or sold where GPU cloud, inference platforms, agent orchestration, or enterprise AI operations made memory and concurrency the argument, not a slide. Warm introductions work better than cold outreach.

Equity and terms

This is serious founding equity. For a true external CEO at this stage it is often mid-teens to low thirties. Cash starts modest or deferred until revenue or a raise. The seat sits on the infrastructure layer next to orchestration and inference, while the product is real and the research is public.

If that trade does not interest you, this role will not either.

How to apply

Send one note. Explain why multi-agent shared-context scarcity matters now, and what you built when DRAM or GPUs were the bottleneck. Link proof.
