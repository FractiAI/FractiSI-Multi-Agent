Open role

Founding CEO

We built the product. We need the person who builds the company.

FractiSI is commercializing Tiny Footprint—software that installs beside the AI stack a customer already runs, so shared context burns less memory and the working set is ready sooner. Local, server, and cloud share one engine. The kit is public. The papers are public. What is not built yet is the commercial machine around it: partners, pilots, pricing that closes, and a raise that does not waste the window.

Memory and accelerator scarcity are not a pitch deck theme right now. They are a daily constraint for anyone serving models at scale. That is the market. We are not recruiting for another assistant product.

The seat

Founding CEO—or Co-founder / CEO if that title fits how you work. The technical founder stays on the invention and the architecture. You own go-to-market, operations, fundraising, and the first hires.

If you would rather stay off the CEO line, COO or Head of GTM works—so long as you have already sold and scaled under real infrastructure pressure. We are not hiring a Chief Scientist as the first executive. That work is already staffed.

What “good” looks like here

You have shipped or sold in GPU cloud, inference platforms, or enterprise AI ops—places where latency, HBM, and fleet cost were the argument, not a slide. Names that often fit: Lambda, Runpod, CoreWeave, Together, Fireworks, Anyscale; NVIDIA or AMD partner / solutions people who left for a company. Warm introductions beat cold outreach.

You want founder equity more than a corporate package. Cash starts modest or deferred until revenue or a raise. Equity for a true external CEO at this stage is meaningful—think mid-teens to low thirties, negotiated with someone who can actually run the company. The first ninety days are concrete: tighten the entity and ops, land credits and early design partners, get one real pilot moving, and put a raise or revenue path on the calendar.

What we already have

A downloadable Workstation, a public research spine, local lab results, and an H100 simulation path aimed at the rhyme case that prefix caches miss. Live partner silicon is next—not a finished fleet proof. We will send a short pack—product note, validation plan, target list—to people who are serious.
